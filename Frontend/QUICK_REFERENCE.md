## 🎓 SEO Basics for You

### What is SEO?

SEO = Search Engine Optimization
It's making your website easy for Google to understand and rank.

### Why Does It Matter?

- 93% of online experiences begin with a search engine
- 75% of people never scroll past page 1
- Local searches lead to visits within 24 hours

### Key SEO Terms

- **Organic Traffic**: Visitors from Google (not ads)
- **Keywords**: Words people search for
- **Rankings**: Your position in search results
- **Local SEO**: Appearing in local area searches
- **Schema**: Code that helps Google understand your site
